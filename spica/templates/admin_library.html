How the DJ is picking

The DJ offers a set from the record table when audio analysis has measured the station and there is a record on air to anchor the first move. Otherwise it offers the artist shelf. Similarity shapes what the shelf leaves out; popularity shapes the hits/deep-cut lean; genres decide what may play at all.
